# Visual decoding pilot

This prototype contains five short blocks. Each block starts with two practice trials followed by six recorded trials. Read the instruction above each chart, use the slider to place the red ball, and select Next when satisfied. You can use the arrow keys to refine your selection.

Practice trials include a button to reveal the target. Test trials provide no correctness feedback.

The study records your selections, their timing, and chart geometry. This prototype does not acquire gaze by itself. The researcher must configure and validate the eye tracker separately.

Use a desktop display and keep the browser zoom and window size consistent throughout the session. The researcher should complete tracker calibration and validation before starting.

This is a development pilot. The study team must supply the applicable participant information and consent before research recruitment.
